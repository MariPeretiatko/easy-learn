# Иллюстрация экрана входа и регистрации

Файл проекта: `public/images/auth-study.webp` (101 090 байт). Создан встроенным инструментом `image_gen` с навыком imagegen; CLI/API fallback не использовался. Исходный PNG сохранён инструментом, проект использует WebP-кодирование того же изображения. На телефоне picture/source не загружает настольную иллюстрацию.

Композиция и формы следуют пользовательским референсам. Это новая иллюстрация, а не извлечённая фотография Quizlet. Название Easy Learn и заголовок выводятся HTML поверх изображения. Шрифт Nunito Sans размещён локально в `public/fonts/nunito-sans/`; лицензия — `OFL.txt` в том же каталоге.

Финальный промпт:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photographic background for the LEFT half of an educational web app login/signup page. Generate a square 1536x1536 premium studio still-life matching this exact composition: a smooth light lavender purple (#d4a7ef) backdrop, slightly darker toward the bottom right. Four overlapping plain school exercise notebooks in a slightly fanned diagonal stack, from back to front saturated hot magenta, coral orange, golden yellow, and bright mint emerald green. The large front green notebook is portrait oriented, rotated about 10 degrees clockwise. A pair of realistic silver-white over-ear wireless headphones rests diagonally across the green notebook, headband arch running around its upper and right edges, left ear cup near middle-left and the right ear cup near the lower center. The whole stack occupies the right and lower two-thirds of the square, cropped generously at the right edge; notebooks start around 24% from left and 27% from top. Keep the upper-left 35% area empty lavender for a large dark headline, and bottom-left 15% empty lavender for a live brand wordmark. View from directly above with slight perspective, realistic paper textures, soft shadows cast down and right, soft studio light. Exactly these objects only. No text, no labels, no logos, no website, no UI, no borders, no letters or numbers. This is only the background asset, not a mockup of the webpage.
